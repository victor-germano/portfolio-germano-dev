About
Who I am

Software Engineer specializing in enterprise backend systems, autonomous AI pipelines, and production APIs. Designed and delivered Grupo Roveri's internal MDM/EMM — approved end-to-end by management, development, and cybersecurity teams.

I build systems that solve real problems: from fiscal agents with atomic idempotency to SAST platforms with AST analysis across 8 languages. 10+ projects in production.

Engineering principles
  • Clean, tested, maintainable code — architecture that scales, not just "works"
  • AI as an engineering component, embedded in the system — not decoration
  • Measurable impact: Cybersecurity-approved systems, SLA compliance in production
  • Continuous learning — technology moves fast, I move faster
Specialties
AI & Autonomous Agents

Multi-agent pipelines with Claude AI — from zero to production. 7-agent sales automation pipeline, enterprise NFS-e fiscal agent, ~1s-latency voice assistant, and a SAST platform with real AST parsing across 8 languages.

Automation & Integrations

Enterprise API integrations (Stripe, SEFAZ, Google Maps, WhatsApp, Gmail OAuth) via N8N and Make. Idempotent flows with retry/backoff, reconciliation, and audit trails — eliminating manual processes with guaranteed SLA.

Backends & Enterprise Systems

Production backends with Node.js/TypeScript, Fastify 5, PostgreSQL 16, and Redis. Enterprise patterns: BullMQ job queues, LGPD audit trails, atomic idempotency, graceful shutdown, and OpenAPI contracts generated from Zod schemas.
